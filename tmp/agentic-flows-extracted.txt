Seamless visit & between-visit care, step by step
A close-up on the visit journey for a patient with diabetes and high blood pressure — pre-visit, during the visit, and after — showing exactly where
Nudge's AI agents act on the patient's behalf.
Marcus is 54, with type 2 diabetes and high blood pressure. He shouldn't have to become his own project manager to get through a visit. In these three
flows, Nudge handles the overlooked details — ordering and scheduling the labs, the fasting window, parking and rides, getting his home blood-
pressure readings and HbA1c into the record, closing quality gaps, refills, monitoring, and escalation. The result is simple: the right information
reaches the right person at the right time, the care team's quality measures are met, and Marcus has less to carry.

Pre-visit — getting Marcus to the lab and into the visit
The month before the appointment. Nudge does the chasing so Marcus doesn't have to: it gets the labs ordered, books the draw around his life, handles
the logistics, and makes sure the physician has everything before he walks in.
Nudge acts in 13 of 16 steps. The muted boxes are other people or systems; the warm boxes are Nudge doing the work.

Post-visit — turning the plan into daily action, and keeping the team in the loop
After the visit, the plan becomes small daily steps. Nudge handles the refills, keeps an eye on his readings, feeds the trends back to the care team,
escalates anything dangerous, and quietly keeps his quality measures current — then rolls it all into the next visit.
